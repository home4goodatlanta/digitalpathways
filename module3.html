Lesson 2 · 90 min Checkpoint pending

Workplace correspondence and interview replies

Strong workplace messages put the main purpose first, confirm exact dates and time zones, and ask missing questions politely. Compare an unclear message with a professional one, then draft your own reply.

Watch me · Before and After

Notice what changes when we write purpose-first

Before · Unclear Draft

Subject: hey

hey i got ur message about next week. yeah i can probably do friday. wait is it on zoom or in person?? thx - jordan

Missing: exact date/time, time zone, polite greeting, and clear subject.

After · Clear & Professional

Subject: Interview Confirmation — Jordan Lee

Hello Ms. Johnson,
Thank you for the interview invitation. I am confirming that I am available on Friday, November 13, 2026, from 2:00 to 2:30 PM Eastern. Could you please share the video meeting link when it is ready?
Best regards,
Jordan Lee

Clear purpose, exact date and time zone, polite single question, respectful closing.

Lesson 3 · 90 min Checkpoint pending

Attachments, links, and email safety

Knowing what to attach—and what never to click—protects both your job search and your personal information. Practice telling safe messages apart from phishing tricks, and compare clean filenames with risky ones.

Watch me · File & Link Habits

File attachments vs. inline links

Item What it does Safe workplace habit
PDF Attachment
Jordan_Lee_Resume.pdf		 Travels inside the email and keeps your résumé layout identical on any device. Preview the file before sending so you never attach an old draft or personal document.
Editable Copy
Jordan_Lee_Resume.docx		 Used when you need to update skills or tailor wording for a new role. Keep in your own folder; send the PDF to employers unless an application asks for `.docx`.
Inline Web Link
https://...		 Opens a web page or portal outside your email app. Never enter a password or ID through an unexpected link. Open the official site yourself instead.

Lesson 4 · Beginner AI Literacy Checkpoint pending

Using AI responsibly as an email assistant

An AI assistant can suggest polite wording for a workplace message, but it can also invent wrong dates, times, or promises with total confidence. You stay in charge: protect your privacy, check every fact against the source, and edit the draft in your own words.

AI Privacy Guardrail — Never Include

Treat any AI prompt box like a public whiteboard. Leave out passwords, Social Security numbers, state IDs, real home addresses, phone numbers, case numbers, or justice-system history. Use fictional placeholders or general descriptions only.

Watch me · The CLEAR Prompt Method

Five steps to a useful, safe prompt

C
Context

What is the situation? (e.g., replying to a fictional orientation note)

L
Let AI know task

What do you want help writing or polishing?

E
Explain details

Which exact facts may it use? Tell it not to invent extras.

A
Ask for format

Short two-sentence email, polite tone, clear sign-off.

R
Review & refine

Compare word-for-word against the source and fix mistakes.
