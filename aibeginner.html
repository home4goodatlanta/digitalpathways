Understanding AI & Everyday Examples

A generative AI tool produces new text or ideas in response to an instruction called a prompt. It works by predicting helpful language patterns—not by looking up verified facts the way a human researcher checks official records. Knowing what AI does well and where human judgment takes over keeps you in control.

Generative AI

Drafts emails, suggests practice interview questions, summarizes notes, and formats lists from the details you give it.

Limit: Can invent confident errors

Search Engines

Finds existing web pages, official agency portals, transit schedules, and published employer websites.

Limit: Still requires checking source trust

Human Expertise

Brings lived judgment, verifies truthfulness, protects privacy, spots job scams, and owns every final workplace message.

Strength: Accountability & care

02 Safety First Not yet practiced

Privacy, Security & Responsible Boundaries

Before typing anything into an AI tool, pause and protect your privacy. Anything typed into an online assistant may be stored or reviewed depending on platform settings. Never share passwords, Social Security numbers, state IDs, bank numbers, home street addresses, or personal justice-system history.

Always Keep Out of Prompts

  • Passwords, PINs, or one-time login codes
  • Social Security numbers, state IDs, or bank information
  • Real home addresses, phone numbers, or birthdates
  • Case numbers, legal history, or medical details

Three AI Risks to Watch For

  • Hallucinations: Confident-sounding wrong dates, times, or fake qualifications.
  • Bias: Unfair assumptions about background, education, or ability.
  • Deceptive Content: Polished phishing emails or fake job listings that mimic real employers.

03 Choosing Your Tool Not yet practiced

Meeting Modern AI Assistants

Workplaces and training centers may permit tools such as Google Gemini, ChatGPT, or Microsoft Copilot—or they may require offline practice on paper. No specific vendor is required to succeed. Just as important: knowing when writing a quick message yourself is faster and safer than opening an AI tool at all.

Option Common Workplace Fit Safety & Access Note
Google Gemini Drafting plain-language emails, structuring interview prep, organizing checklists alongside Docs or Gmail. Use only site-approved accounts; keep personal identifiers out of prompts.
ChatGPT Brainstorming résumé action verbs, practicing mock interview dialogues, simplifying dense instructions. Review every claim; verify dates, names, and job qualifications against your source.
Microsoft Copilot Drafting workplace notes, summaries, and Word/Outlook drafts inside licensed office setups. Follow employer data rules; confirm whether organizational privacy protections apply.
Manual / Offline Writing Short 1–2 sentence confirmations, confidential entries, or any task where facts are already clear. Fastest, zero-risk option when you already know exactly what to say.
