Step 1 • Watch / Guide Lesson 5.1

How to Navigate Job Boards and Filter Real Opportunities

Job boards list hundreds of openings, including sponsored ads and occasional scams. Using filters helps you focus on roles that fit your schedule, pay needs, and real strengths.

Job Posting

An employer's notice listing the role title, duties, schedule, pay, and qualifications.

Applicant Tracking System (ATS)

Software employers use to collect online applications and scan résumés for matching keywords.

Application Portal

The secure employer webpage where you create a login or upload your résumé to apply.

Required vs. Preferred

Required skills are essential; preferred skills are helpful extras, not strict deal-breakers.

Step 2 • Let's Do It Lessons 5.1 & 5.2

Spot Scam Red Flags and Match Your Transferable Skills

Before you share your name or upload a résumé, pause and check two things: Is this employer legitimate, and which of your real skills match what they actually need?

Verified Employer Signs

What Safe Hiring Looks Like

  • Official domain: Emails and portals match the organization's real website (in training, reserved .test domains like hr@riverside-example.test).
  • Zero upfront cost: Employers never charge a fee for an interview, background check, or training software.
  • Clear job details: Realistic pay range, specific everyday duties, and an identifiable workplace location.
  • Respects privacy: Never asks for your SSN, bank account, or ID photo over text before a formal offer.
Warning Signs

Common Job Scam Red Flags

  • Pay-to-play fees: Requests for application fees, gift cards, crypto, or "starter kit" payments.
  • Urgent credential links: Unexpected texts or emails demanding you click a link within 30 minutes to verify your password or ID.
  • Too-good-to-be-true pay: High hourly pay ($40+/hr) for vague "easy remote entry" with zero interview.
  • Look-alike addresses: Slightly altered domains like riverside-urgent.example.test instead of the official domain.

Responsible AI Practice • Module 7 Bridge

Compare Job Keywords with AI — Without Fabricating Experience

An AI tool can help spot matching keywords between a job description and your skills list. However, AI can also guess or invent work history you do not have. Use the CLEAR method and keep you — the human — in charge.

C
Context

Share only the fictional role and general skills.

L
Let AI Know Task

Ask it to compare keywords, not write fake history.

E
Explain Details

State: "Use only the facts listed. Do not invent jobs."

A
Ask for Format

Request a short bulleted list of matches and gaps.

R
Review & Refine

Check every word against facts and cut untrue claims.
