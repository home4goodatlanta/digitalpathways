How Tabs & Controls Help You

Tabs let you keep two pages open side-by-side so you can compare information without losing your place. Try clicking the second tab, hitting the Refresh button, or clicking the Star icon to save a trusted page to your bookmarks bar.

1.2 Address Bar vs. Search Bar — and How to Read a Web Address

Address Bar (Top Box) Takes you directly to a known site

If you type an exact address like careeronestop.org into the top bar, you go straight to that organization’s official website — skipping paid ads.

Search Bar (Middle of Page) Finds matching pages across the web

Typing words like “workplace safety Atlanta” shows a list of results. The top results are often paid Sponsored ads, not always the safest official site.

1.3 Does the Padlock Prove a Website Is Safe?

Quick Check: You see a small padlock icon (HTTPS) next to a website address. Does that padlock alone prove the organization is honest and legitimate?

Common Domain Extensions & Trust Signals

.gov — Government

Reserved for official U.S. government agencies (like consumer.ftc.gov or cisa.gov). Cannot be bought by random individuals.

.edu — Education

Used by accredited colleges and universities. Helpful for verified training guides and adult learning programs.

.org — Organizations

Often used by nonprofits and community services. Anyone can register a .org, so still check the About Us and Contact pages.

.com & .test

.com is open to anyone for business or scams — verify carefully. .test is a reserved practice domain used safely in this course.
