Orientation & Safety Promise

Start here — even if you have never used AI before

Everyone starts this workshop at Level 1. There is no placement test, no rush, and no grading based on your background. Our guiding rhythm is simple: Watch how it works → Practice together → Try on your own → Check what is true.

The Non-Negotiable Safety Rule

Zero Real Personal Information in AI Prompts

Never type real names, Social Security numbers, state IDs, passwords, case numbers, phone numbers, home addresses, bank details, or immigration records into an AI tool or training form.

Instead, we practice safely with fictional people and reserved placeholder addresses—like Jordan Lee applying to Riverside Community Services using jordan.lee@riverside-example.test.

Psychological Safety & Dignity

Your judgment stays in charge

An AI assistant can suggest helpful wording for an email or practice interview. It can also give a confident answer with the wrong time, date, or requirements.

You decide what to keep, what to fix, and what to delete. Participation never requires sharing personal or justice-system history, and you may use large text, paper notes, or extended pacing whenever you need.

Section 1 • Lesson 7.1 Foundations

What generative AI is — and how it differs from search

Generative AI produces new text (like a draft message or list of questions) in response to an instruction called a prompt. It predicts helpful patterns of words—it does not look up live private schedules or guarantee facts the way an official source does.

Capabilities • Where AI Helps

Strong starting partner

  • Drafting a polite reply when you supply the exact facts
  • Creating 5 practice interview questions for a target role
  • Simplifying confusing workplace instructions into clear steps
  • Brainstorming presentation outlines, storyboards, or study quizzes
Boundaries • Where AI Falls Short

Cannot replace verification

  • Does not know your real appointment room, time, or supervisor unless you provide it
  • Can invent certificates, dates, or job requirements ("hallucinations")
  • Cannot keep secrets if you paste private credentials into public tools
  • Cannot verify whether a job posting or login link is a scam

Section 2 • Safety, Privacy & Human Responsibility

Protect private information and spot confident errors

AI tools can sound completely certain even when they make up facts, reflect unfair bias, or echo unsafe advice. The Human Responsibility Rule means you stay accountable for every message you send and every detail you share.

Data Boundary 1

Never Paste PII or Credentials

Keep Social Security numbers, driver's license or state ID numbers, passwords, PINs, case numbers, real client files, and proprietary employer documents out of AI prompts.

Data Boundary 2

Watch for Hallucinations & Bias

A hallucination is when AI invents a detail—like adding a certification you do not hold or changing Tuesday 9:00 a.m. to Thursday full-morning. Biased outputs can also stereotype applicants or overstate experience.

Data Boundary 3

The Human Responsibility Rule

Never copy-paste an AI answer straight into an application or email. Check every fact against your source document, remove fabricated claims, and rewrite in your own natural voice.

Section 3 • Meet Permitted AI Tools

Choosing the right assistant—or doing a task manually

Depending on your training site or workplace, you may see tools like ChatGPT, Google Gemini, or Microsoft Copilot—or practice offline using printed examples. Live tool access is never required to learn these skills.

ChatGPT (OpenAI)

A widely used conversational assistant accessible via browser or mobile app. Useful for practicing interview role-play, brainstorming résumé action verbs, and drafting outlines from fictional notes.

Good Workplace Fit

Structured role-play, rewriting unclear drafts into polite plain language, and formatting checklists.

Safety & Verification Habit

Check privacy settings before signing in, never enter real credentials, and verify any dates or claims it writes.

Google Gemini

Google's conversational assistant that works in a browser and integrates with Google Workspace tools when enabled by a site. Helpful for organizing study notes, comparing options, and drafting clear workplace emails.

Good Workplace Fit

Summarizing provided training articles, drafting clear correspondence, and outlining presentations.

Safety & Verification Habit

Keep personal account passwords private, use approved training accounts, and cross-check any factual claims against primary websites.

Microsoft Copilot

Microsoft's AI assistant available on the web and within Microsoft 365 environments when licensed. Useful for organizing documents, outlining Word résumés, and drafting polite scheduling replies.

Good Workplace Fit

Formatting workplace memos, checking email tone, and turning bullet notes into structured tables.

Safety & Verification Habit

Verify whether you are in an organization-protected session or a public browser session before entering any internal work context.

Offline & Paper Simulation (Zero Connectivity Needed)

When internet access or AI platforms are restricted at a facility, learners practice the exact same CLEAR prompting and verification steps using printed worksheets (7A and 7B) and sample AI drafts.

Good Workplace Fit

Building prompt-writing habits, spotting fabricated details, and practicing human revision without any account or login.

Safety & Verification Habit

Zero risk of data leakage; teaches the core critical-reading habit that works on every future AI tool.

Section 4 • Hands-On CLEAR Prompt Simulator

Write better prompts with the CLEAR framework

A vague prompt gets a vague or inaccurate reply. Use the five CLEAR steps to give the AI assistant exact fictional facts, set clear limits, and keep yourself in control of the final wording.

C
Context — What is the situation? (Example: "I am preparing for an entry-level customer service interview.")
L
Let AI know the task — What do you want help with? (Example: "Create five practice interview questions.")
E
Explain details — What facts may it use, and what makes it useful? (Example: "Use simple language and focus on teamwork and reliability.")
A
Ask for a format — How should the answer look? (Example: "Give a numbered list with short example answers.")
R
Review & refine — What needs checking or fixing? (Example: "Remove assumptions about my work history and simplify question 3.")

Section 5 • Employment & AI Ready Adventures

Practical workplace tasks—and creative ways to build skills

Beyond simple chat replies, AI can help you prepare for interviews, polish truthful résumé statements, draft work emails, and explore creative projects—like presentation outlines, short video storyboards, and study quiz games—as long as you supply the facts and verify the output.

Interview Preparation — Customer Support Associate

Safe CLEAR Prompt

"Jordan Lee is preparing for a Customer Support Associate interview at Riverside Community Services. Using only volunteer front desk registration and retail stock experience, give 3 behavioral questions and brief talking points about active listening and organized records."

Simulated AI Draft + Human Check

Q1: Tell me about a time you kept records organized under busy conditions.

Draft point: "At my volunteer front desk role, I checked in community guests and kept registration logs orderly so follow-up went smoothly."

Human check: Truthful—matches Jordan's 2025–2026 volunteer experience without exaggerating job titles.

Résumé Refinement — Keep Every Word Truthful

Safe CLEAR Prompt

"Rewrite these two fictional notes into two clear résumé bullets for a Customer Support role: (1) helped people sign in at community events, (2) stocked shelves and tracked inventory. Do not add management titles or software tools not listed."

Simulated AI Draft + Human Check

• Welcomed guests at community event front desk and maintained accurate sign-in records.
• Organized stockroom inventory and assisted team members with daily shelf restocking.

Human check: Accurate, plain-language verbs, zero invented software certifications, saved in both editable (.docx) and .pdf formats.

Professional Email Drafting — Asking for Missing Details

Source Message (Module 3 Scenario)

From: Ms. Johnson, Training Supervisor
"Hello Jordan, please confirm that you can attend orientation on Thursday, November 12, 2026, at 10:00 AM Eastern. Send your sample résumé."

Human-Verified Draft

Subject: Orientation Confirmation — Jordan Lee
"Hello Ms. Johnson, thank you for the orientation details. I confirm I will attend on Thursday, November 12, 2026, at 10:00 AM Eastern. Could you please share the meeting location? I have attached my sample résumé. Thank you, Jordan Lee."

Human check: Confirms exact date/time, politely asks for the missing location, and reminds you to verify the attached Jordan_Lee_Resume.pdf file before clicking send.

AI Ready Adventures — Beyond Chatbots

In our classroom storyline, skeptical learners discover that AI can support visual presentations, short video storyboards, simple learning games, and study helpers—not just emails. Creative outputs are still drafts that humans review and refine.

3. Practice Quiz Game Concept
