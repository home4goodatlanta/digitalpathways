1 Lesson 6.1 • Pre-Join Check & Link Safety Not started

Verify the meeting link before you enter the room

A virtual interview starts five to ten minutes before the clock hits your scheduled time. Before clicking any link, check who sent it, confirm it matches your calendar invitation, and preview your microphone and display name in the pre-join screen.

Key rule on links and safety: Legitimate employers never ask you to pay an interview fee, send gift cards, or enter your bank or ID numbers to unlock a meeting link. Keep private interview links off social media so uninvited people cannot join your session.

Even in professional offices, audio cuts out, browser permissions block a camera, or Wi-Fi slows down. How you respond—staying calm, trying one fix, and updating the interviewer—shows workplace professionalism.

5 Lesson 6.2 • Scheduling & Time Zones 0 of 3 checks passed

Read calendar invitations and time zones accurately

Remote interviews often list the employer’s time zone—such as Eastern Time—even if you live or work in another zone. Always convert the time before you RSVP, set a 15-minute reminder, and confirm any missing location details.

6 Module 7 Bridge • CLEAR Prompts, STAR & Fact-Checking In progress

Use AI to prepare—without giving away privacy or inventing facts

An AI assistant can generate practice interview questions and help organize your ideas. However, AI often invents accomplishments or dates you never had. Use the CLEAR prompt framework, protect private data, and verify every word against your real history.

CLEAR Prompting Guide (Module 7)
  • C — Context: What is the situation? (“Preparing for an entry-level customer support interview.”)
  • L — Let AI know the task: What help do you want? (“Suggest a STAR practice draft.”)
  • E — Explain details: Share only safe, true facts from Jordan’s card.
  • A — Ask for a format: Request simple plain-language bullets.
  • R — Review & refine: Remove anything AI invented and rewrite in your own voice.
